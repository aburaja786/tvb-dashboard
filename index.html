P&L vs Expense Report — Gap Analysis

The P&L is accrual-based (cost when consumed), while the Expense Report is cash-based (when actually paid). Some difference is normal due to timing — bills paid late, deposits, advance salary. But large persistent gaps need attention.

Total expense difference over 10 months: OMR 4,107.61 — Expense Report shows OMR 64,864 vs P&L OMR 68,971. This means roughly OMR 411/month in P&L expenses is not yet reflected in actual cash payments — i.e. accrued but unpaid.
